# ADR-029 — Transactional AI spending reservations and reconciliation

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-10-29

**Decision owners:** Product owner, implementation reviewer and security/privacy reviewer

**Related requirements:** `FR-BUD-01–04`, `FR-AI-01/04/06`, `FR-DAT-06–07/12–15`, `AC-DAT-01/04/12–16`, `NFR-REL-02`, `NFR-MNT-01–04`

**Related decisions:** D2/D4/D5; Q1/Q7/Q8; ADR-013/014/015/019/027/028

**Owner acceptance:** 2026-10-29 — **“Yes accepted”** to ADR-027/028/029 and final R1 deletion clarification after Claude Opus 5.5's re-check cleared F1–F13. Option A and the stated v1 accounting/engineering policies are accepted. The discussion history records earlier pending choices; final acceptance supersedes them. Provider/model/tariff/profile/migration/concurrency evidence, independent implementation gates and exact live-call authority remain future requirements. ADR-027/028 are also Accepted.

## Context and options considered

D5 originally selected USD $200/month and $0.25/request plus the separate $1 cumulative first-live-test cap. The owner's editable-token/spending clarification makes normal monthly/request amounts initial defaults that users may deliberately raise/lower, recorded in specification v0.4 under `FR-BUD-01`; the distinct $1 development-test authority remains. Effective configured amounts are app ceilings, not targets, credits or account-wide controls. D4 excludes saved prompts and separates accounting from 30-day content. The owner has now accepted the detailed Q7 design below; actual accounting/provider implementation evidence remains pending.

| Option | Advantages | Costs and risks |
|---|---|---|
| A — Local transactional worst-case reservation, one attempt and conservative reconciliation (**recommended**) | Deterministic caps across processes/crashes; no billing/admin credential or background API needed. | Uncertain outcomes tie up allowance; lost/restored accounting can block AI until continuity is established. |
| B — Deduct estimated spend after receiving a response | Simple happy path. | Concurrent/crashed requests spend before accounting, making caps unenforceable. Rejected. |
| C — Rely on provider credits/dashboard limits or query account billing before each request | Potential extra account-level visibility. | Not app-specific proof, may lag, needs wider authority and cannot replace pre-send reservations. Not adopted; owner-controlled provider controls may supplement A without claimed enforcement. |

## Decision

Accept A: Application accounting ports, pure Domain arithmetic/state transitions and SQLite-owned atomic units of work under ADR-015. AI stays blocked unless exact payload consent, protected key, reviewed model/pricing, enforceable token/output/attempt bounds and sufficient committed allowance all hold. No network balance/usage query, credit purchase, recharge, grant assumption, automatic paid retry or account administration is added.

### Q7 — Accepted currency, period and configuration policy

| Policy | Accepted contract |
|---|---|
| Currency/arithmetic | USD only; checked fixed-point integer micro-USD (1,000,000 units/$), no floating point. Round each computed charge/reservation upward; reject overflow, negative/non-finite/unknown values or currency mismatch. No silent currency conversion. |
| Month | **Owner selected: UTC calendar month, with local-time display.** Half-open `[00:00 UTC on first day, next first day)`, identified durably by year/month; host time-zone/DST changes do not move it. |
| Attribution | Charge the app period whose reservation was committed before dispatch, including a response/late usage arriving after rollover. One approved request has one immutable period/pricing/model binding. This may differ from provider invoice attribution; disclose that difference. |
| Clock/rollover | Persist last accepted accounting UTC/period. Backward movement across a period or unexplained large jump blocks new sends pending owner review; treat a forward wall-clock jump exceeding 24 hours beyond monotonic elapsed time as suspicious while running. After downtime, present a boundary-crossing confirmation before minting a new period. Never reopen an old period or replay sends. This is conservative local tamper/error detection, not trusted time against a compromised user/OS. |
| Pricing | **Owner selected: verified pricing expires within 24 hours; no automatic refresh; missing/expired pricing blocks paid AI.** Explicit versioned USD rate card for the exact model revision/route/service tier, with official source, verified-at/effective/expiry times and every billable category. No silent alias substitution, model switch, regional/tier premium or account discount assumption. Unknown/ambiguous rates also block sends. Verification is a reviewed implementation/owner action, not an extra API call per request. |
| Retries/concurrency | ADR-028 owner selection: manual retries only and one in-flight request. Recommend one HTTP attempt and enforcing the concurrency limit across current-user app processes. A manual retry is a new consented logical request/reservation; uncertain prior charges stay committed. Even with serialized sends, SQLite must atomically reject concurrent reservations. |
| Budget changes | **Owner selected: normal per-request and monthly spending limits are editable upward/downward**, initially USD $0.25/request and $200/month, with no additional product-level dollar maxima. Validate finite non-negative exactly representable USD values using checked arithmetic; reject unsupported precision/overflow rather than silently round settings. When both caps are positive, require request <= month. Either zero disables paid calls and preserves the other value. Show old/new amounts and commitments on explicit save. Changes are prospective/versioned, never automatic on exhaustion; no refund/period/test reset. Lowering below commitments preserves them and blocks new sends until allowance is sufficient. Reset-to-defaults changes settings only; $1 test authority remains separate. |

SDK model IDs/prices are not selected or checked by this ADR. The owner-selected 24-hour maximum local freshness window is not a guarantee prices cannot change inside it. A provider charging above the proven bound is a reconciliation breach: record the full cost, block further paid calls and show that app estimates cannot guarantee provider invoices. Models whose complete worst-case cost cannot be bounded remain unavailable. User-configurable token ceilings remain bounded and must fit a full conservative reservation; a settings change never silently increases a monetary cap or alters an already dispatched request.

An explicit budget save invalidates unsent previews; fresh approval/reservation uses the new configured amounts plus all current commitments. Previously dispatched work retains its original immutable request cap, pricing and reservation policy; a later reduction cannot retroactively make that send unauthorized or remove its potential charge. Display active commitments that exceed a newly lowered setting rather than hide them. Abort/release a reserved-but-unclaimed request only with transactional proof of no dispatch; otherwise retain uncertainty. Tests must cover both directions of edits, zero/invalid values, lowering below spend, raising after exhaustion without refunding old commitments, same-period changes, default reset and concurrent edit/reserve/send boundaries.

### Manual pricing re-confirmation — review F6

Use an **AI pricing** panel showing exact model revision, approved route/tier, USD units/rates for every allowed billing category, last owner-confirmed UTC, 24-hour expiry, and links to the applicable official pricing/model pages. On expiry, paid AI is unavailable while core features remain usable. The user may deliberately open those links, compare the displayed rate card, edit necessary prices, then explicitly **Confirm current prices**. Opening a link, accepting an app update or toggling AI is not confirmation. There is no automatic fetch, hidden API/model query, provider-account access or scheduled refresh.

Each confirmation validates completeness/currency/units/finite checked rate values, records a new immutable tariff version and owner-confirmation timestamp, and starts at most 24 hours of validity. A free/zero rate requires explicit evidence for that exact allowed category, never a blank default. A changed/reconfirmed tariff invalidates unsent previews; in-flight requests keep their frozen rates. Label this **owner-confirmed pricing**, not software-verified current pricing or an authoritative provider bill. The UI states prices can change and manual entry can be wrong; if the owner does not re-confirm, AI stays blocked after expiry. Mocks cover expiry, incomplete/zero/invalid rates, owner edits/attestation, clock discontinuity and confirmation/reservation races. Current model/rates and any future automation still require separate implementation/owner decisions; this is the mechanism for the already selected manual 24-hour policy.

### Reservation arithmetic and invariants

For one approved logical request, calculate `R` as the sum of upward-rounded worst-case charges for every allowed billable category across every allowed attempt. The approved input includes fixed instructions, edited evidence, token framing and SDK payload overhead. Use uncached input rates unless a larger cache-write/long-context/tier rate can apply, in which case reserve that larger valid bound or reject the configuration. Include all billed output/reasoning within the enforceable total; never double-count an included reasoning subcategory. No hosted tools, storage, images/audio, background processing or other unbounded/additional charged capability is enabled. Unknown tokenizer/framing/tariff or unenforceable output bound means **no request**.

In a serialized write transaction, require both `R <= requestCap` and `settledCostThisPeriod + outstandingReservationsThisPeriod + R <= monthlyCap`. During first-live-test mode also require `settledTestCost + outstandingTestReservations + R <= $1`, even across month boundaries, restarts and repeated attempts; ending/restarting the app or reconnecting never resets test spend. First-live-test mode cannot auto-promote to normal budget mode. Fail closed if the reservation/dispatch-intent commit or durability check fails. Store the checked monetary/token bounds and immutable policy/rate version with a unique app request ID, not prompt text, hash or secret.

At most one durable dispatch claim can transition that request to a network attempt. Use transaction/state compare-and-set plus a cross-process operation gate coordinated with the existing data-operation/reset lease. Specify lock ordering and async-safe ownership before implementation: the existing reset mutex is thread-affine and must not simply be held across an arbitrary `await`. Active network work blocks/reset-arms only after local operation state is durable; no process restart automatically resumes a request. Records loaded from SQLite never grant send authority; fresh in-memory payload approval is always needed.

### Crash-safe state machine and reconciliation

| Durable state / boundary | Allowed next step and accounting effect |
|---|---|
| Approved in memory, no reservation | Cannot send. Cancellation costs nothing; a failed commit cannot reach transport. |
| `Reserved`, no committed dispatch claim | Reserve `R` before any transport access. A transactionally proven local abort with no dispatch claim may mark `NotSent` and release `R`. Cancellation alone is not that proof. |
| `DispatchClaimed` committed, before/after HTTP send | Keep the full `R`. A crash in either window is ambiguous; restart marks `Uncertain`, never resends or releases merely because no response ID exists. |
| Well-formed terminal response plus complete reliable usage (including provider-terminal incomplete answer) | Validate request/model/usage binding and totals. In one transaction insert unique usage, calculate actual cost from frozen rates, replace `R` with that cost and mark `Settled`. Release only the proven unused portion. A response stopped at its token limit, refusal or terminal error can still be billable and settle if usage is reliable; answer completeness/storage success is not the charge rule. |
| Timeout, cancellation after claim, connection/redirect/HTTP failure, transport-truncated/malformed/oversized body, or missing/unreliable usage | If complete reliable usage is unavailable, mark `Uncertain` with a sanitized reason and retain `R`. A complete parsed response with reliable terminal usage follows the settlement row instead; the word incomplete in provider answer status does not itself imply missing usage. Do not infer free service from status, exception, missing body or elapsed time. Core UI stays usable. |
| Duplicate/conflicting/out-of-order usage | Unique app-request/provider-response/usage identities prevent duplicate settlement. Identical repeats are no-ops; conflicting or unbound evidence blocks reconciliation/new calls pending review. Do not replace higher commitment with an unproven lower value. |
| Reported charge exceeds `R` or unexpected billing category | Record the full known charge, flag cap/bound breach and block new paid calls. Never clamp the ledger to the reservation or hide provider liability. |

A `Reserved` abort races with no sender because the same database claim/gate arbitrates both. After a dispatch claim, only explicit reliable charge/non-billing evidence can reduce the commitment. No automatic reconciliation network job or billing-admin API is introduced. Preserve per-request usage from a valid response; otherwise keep the reservation unless exact request-bound evidence establishes cost/non-billing. Aggregate totals, a guessed zero, a delay or a fresh key are insufficient. Such external evidence may not be obtainable, so no routine manual recovery is promised. Unknown commitments can remain indefinitely in their original period; a new month's fresh allocation does not refund/delete old liability. A few unknown charges can exhaust the cumulative $1 first-test allowance permanently until separately resolved/authorized. Explain this before initial connection and the later approved Task 71 test plan. Any live reconciliation integration needs separate API/security/credential authority.

### Provider incomplete answer versus interrupted transport — review F4

Official [OpenAI reasoning guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning), checked 2026-10-29, documents that the output/context limit can produce an incomplete result, potentially before visible text, while input/reasoning still incurs cost. The [Responses reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/python/resources/responses/methods/create) defines the response status/output-limit contract. This supports distinguishing answer truncation from an unreadable/interrupted HTTP body; it does **not** prove every incomplete response includes complete usage. Only validate-and-settle when that individual terminal response actually supplies reliable complete usage for the approved model/shape. Otherwise retain uncertainty.

Show **Answer incomplete — output limit reached** (or the appropriate typed reason), any safe partial text and settled/uncertain cost independently. The user may explicitly increase output/spending limits if desired, then approve a fresh request; no automatic retry or silent budget increase. Storage failure/not-saved status also remains independent of usage settlement. Fixtures must include complete usage with no text, complete usage with partial text, absent/malformed/conflicting usage, transport cutoff before usage, and usage/charge above reservation. Reasoning-heavy models may produce little within the recommended 1,024 default; no model is declared useful at that default without later evidence.

### Ending first-live-test mode

The development-only $1 cumulative envelope remains active across calls/months/restarts until the exact owner-approved Task 71 evidence is reviewed and the owner separately authorizes ending test mode for ordinary use. No automatic promotion on a successful response, budget edit, expiry or app reset. Preserve original test totals/uncertainty after leaving the mode; they are not refunded, and current-month commitments still count normally. Missing test continuity remains blocked under the declared recovery policy. This is a later explicit gate outcome, not a new ordinary-user spending setting and not authority for a paid call in Task 46.

Clarify R6: the **test-only versus ordinary-use capability is selected by reviewed build/release policy**, not a user-editable app-data flag. Test request costs/reservations still live in the app ledger and are removed by complete reset; a build flag alone cannot recover erased spend or detect external rollback. Missing test continuity and a no-prior-use declaration do not create new development-test authority: each Task 71 paid test still requires the owner's exact current test plan/cumulative-envelope check outside ordinary settings. Changing budgets, reinstalling or resetting never authorizes promotion; ordinary use requires the separately approved artifact/policy after evidence review. No hidden surviving reset marker, new installer or release action is introduced in Task 46.

### Monthly rollover without releasing uncertainty

A new confirmed UTC month starts a fresh monthly ceiling for **new** requests. Old settlements and reservations stay in their original period; they neither vanish nor consume that new period's allocation again. Show outstanding liability across periods separately, so a new-month display cannot imply “no previous charges”. Late settlement changes only its original period and the cumulative first-test ledger. No rollover refunds a request, lifts the user-configured per-request cap, replenishes the $1 first-test cap or proves that the provider considers a charge paid/free.

### Minimal records, retention and lifecycle

Persist only app request/reservation ID, accounting epoch/period, approved provider/model/tariff/policy identifiers, UTC transition timestamps, token/output bounds and reported counts, checked reservation/actual micro-USD, typed outcome/reconciliation reason, and minimal opaque provider request/response IDs needed for duplicate/reconciliation proof. No prompt/evidence, body hash, key, account email, machine/user identity, arbitrary provider object or raw response/error. Provider IDs are accounting metadata and never silently exported/sent back to the model.

**Owner selected:** detailed settled spending records for the current and previous 12 UTC months, then older settled-record compaction into monthly totals with duplicate-prevention evidence; unresolved charges remain until reconciled. Recommend implementing that compaction transactionally, preserving exact settled totals, compact unique usage identities and immutable per-request tariff references needed for disputes. Do not claim a fixed storage bound for indefinite uncertainty. Unresolved reservations and their binding/tariff/dedup evidence persist until reliably reconciled; a row-count/storage ceiling must block new calls instead of deleting commitments. The accepted **10,000 detailed-row / 16 MiB logical-ledger soft ceiling** applies, with visible maintenance/blocking and measured physical storage limits required before shipping. D4's 30 days applies only to content, not accounting.

Scan/download/log/history clear and provider disconnect/key replacement preserve accounting. Under the owner's F5 choice, scan clear removes diagnostic-timeline explanations and preserves failed-update explanations; update-history clear removes failed-update explanations and preserves the diagnostic kind. Both have their own normal content expiry and are deleted by reset. Purging either explanation kind or a snapshot cannot cascade-delete its reservation/usage or create a refund. Coordinate ADR-027's required provenance and separate logical categories with ADR-028's saved-content schema. Recognized SQLite migration/backup recovery preserves monetary states atomically or blocks AI for continuity review; an externally substituted stale copy may be undetectable as disclosed below. All ledger values are validated as untrusted reads, never treated as authority to send.

### Reset, restore and missing accounting continuity

**Owner selected after F3:** Factory reset deletes all app-owned ledger/reservation state and the exact protected credential, with no surviving spending counter, reset flag or external epoch marker. It preserves the existing first-launch semantics; the next instance cannot distinguish a completed reset from a genuinely new installation by app state alone. Before reset confirmation, disclose that deletion removes local spending knowledge and does not cancel provider charges. Do not claim a mechanically enforced post-reset spending block that depends on evidence the reset removed.

At first AI connection with no established accounting continuity, explicitly ask whether PC Steward has previously made AI requests on this PC, or prior/restored state may exist. Label the answer as the user's declaration. **No prior use** permits establishing the current-period ledger only on that declaration and all normal consent/pricing/reservation gates. **Yes or unsure** blocks paid calls until either reliable current-period commitments are reconstructed under an explicit owner-reviewed recovery record or a confirmed next UTC month establishes a fresh future-call period; disclose unknown earlier liability. Persist the declaration only in the new app state, which a later Factory reset also deletes. Do not silently default to no prior use. Re-entering a key, restoring default budgets or raising a cap is not continuity evidence.

Limit mechanically recognized stale-state recovery to recovery PC Steward itself initiates/recognizes, such as its existing pre-migration-backup recovery or detected missing/corrupt accounting. Those paths disable AI and require the same fresh declaration/continuity review before new paid calls. This is a recovery guard, not a new general-purpose backup/restore feature. **External file restores** through File History, OneDrive, manual database replacement or whole-machine snapshots may restore both ledger and settings silently and **cannot reliably be detected** by this design. Disclose that limitation in AI setup/recovery guidance; do not claim the ledger is authoritative across undetected external restores or a compromised user/OS. No surviving reset marker is added to solve that different threat model.

No automatic account query or inference from aggregate provider credit is allowed. Reliable request-bound charge evidence is conditional and may be unavailable; do not promise automatic reconstruction. If prior $1 first-live-test spending is unknown, also block live-test re-enablement until reconstructed or a separate new test authority is explicitly given; a month boundary or a no-prior-use declaration is not a refund of known test usage. During a deliberate first-connection flow, an orphaned exact PC Steward credential is evidence of possible prior state and must trigger the yes/unsure path rather than silent reconnection; no secret-store enumeration or credential probe is added to normal core startup. Multiple installs/Windows users cannot be claimed to share one app cap without a shared reviewed ledger. Core features remain available when only AI accounting continuity is unresolved.

## Consequences and required evidence

Conservative accounting can block useful calls after a timeout, price expiry or recovery. UTC periods avoid DST-dependent windows; the owner must accept that billing invoices may use different periods. A single attempt reduces cost ambiguity but needs explicit SDK retry tests. The app cannot cap other applications' spending, prevent external credit purchases, promise grant-only use or guarantee a provider's invoice. No account balance or promotional amount is asserted.

Accepted additive 2.0 extensions cover ADR-015 atomic reservation/dispatch/usage, retained accounting/dedup evidence, recovery barriers and compaction; ADR-013/019/reset design ledger deletion and honest first-connection/recognized-recovery re-enablement; ADR-014 isolated accounting/transport ports. Earlier documents describe the existing baseline; Task 47/relevant implementation tasks must carry these accepted contracts and align detailed design/evidence before the new capability ships. No source behavior changes here.

Tasks 68–70 must prove exact-cap/one-unit-over boundaries, checked arithmetic/rounding, unknown/stale/currency/tier/alias prices, input/output/framing costs, retries disabled, concurrent app processes, commit/send/result crash windows, no claimed-reservation replay, cancellation races, error/refusal/missing/duplicate/conflicting usage, oversize responses, charge-above-reservation and full-core fallback. Fixed-clock fixtures cover UTC/DST boundaries, clock rollback/jumps, late reconciliation and cumulative test spend across months. Migration/compaction/history clears, key rotation, missing/corrupt/partial/stale restore, reset during request, external accounting reconstruction and no post-reset test-cap reset need synthetic fixtures. Build/full suite and independent security/privacy/cost gate precede Task 71's separate exact live-call approval. No new tests/code, paid call, provider balance read or budget-setting change occurs in Task 46.

## Reconsider when

Owner chooses another accounting period/currency/retention, supported pricing cannot produce a complete conservative bound, provider evidence cannot support useful reconciliation, or continuity/compaction proofs fail. Keep paid AI blocked rather than silently weaken caps or release uncertain commitments.
